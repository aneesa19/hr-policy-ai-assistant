COMPANY HR POLICY DOCUMENT

1. VACATION POLICY
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation days accrue at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days to the next calendar year. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and require manager approval.

2. SICK LEAVE POLICY  
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or to care for immediate family members. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY
The company offers 16 weeks of paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 6 weeks for secondary caregivers. This applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement. Employees must have completed 12 months of service to be eligible.

4. PAID LEAVE POLICY
In addition to vacation and sick leave, employees receive 3 paid personal days per year for any purpose. Personal days do not carry over to the next year.

5. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees are eligible for hybrid work arrangements with 2-3 days per week in the office and remaining days remote. Fully remote positions are available for select roles with manager approval.

6. HEALTH BENEFITS
The company provides comprehensive health insurance including medical, dental, and vision coverage. Coverage begins on the first day of employment. Family coverage is available at subsidized rates.

7. RETIREMENT BENEFITS
Employees are eligible to participate in the company 401k plan after 90 days of employment. The company matches 100% of contributions up to 5% of salary.

8. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Each employee receives an annual professional development budget of $2000 for conferences, courses, certifications, and books related to their role.

9. WORK HOURS
Standard work hours are 9 AM to 5 PM Monday through Friday. Flexible scheduling is available with manager approval. Overtime for non-exempt employees is compensated at 1.5 times regular hourly rate.

10. CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior, respect diverse perspectives, and uphold the highest ethical standards. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited.
